## 📖 Description
**Nice Things** adds a bunch of new stuff - items, blocks, recipes, and small tweaks to make the game a bit more fun. From Conveyor Blocks to chain climbing, there's plenty to mess around with! Suggestions are always welcome.

## 🧭 Detailed Overview
<details>
<summary>Display Case</summary>
A new block that can be crafted. It displays and rotates an Item given to. Can only be used/broken by the player who placed it. 17 different variants available.
</details>
<details>
<summary>Conveyor</summary>
A new block that can be crafted. It transports Items and Entities laying on it in one direction and turns Dead Corals into living Corals, Concrete Powder into Concrete and Dirt into Mud when it swoops through water while on a Conveyor Block.
</details>
<details>
<summary>Fan</summary>
A new craftable block that fires Items and Entities in the direction it is facing. It also ages Copper Blocks when they are being fired.
</details>
<details>
<summary>Chopper</summary>
Functions much like the Conveyor but also cuts and breaks some blocks into others. Like Stone into Cobblestone, Logs into their Stripped Variants, Ore Blocks will be broken down into the ores.
</details>
<details>
<summary>Container</summary>
A new storage block that can be crafted. It stores up to 10000 stackable items of one type. (It also makes for a neat Item filter.)
</details>
<details>
<summary>Copper Golem</summary>
Players can now give Copper Golems a variety of Items they want them to wear on their Rods by throwing them the item.

Full List available here: https://github.com/Explorers-Eden/Nice-Things/blob/main/data/minecraft/tags/item/shearable_from_copper_golem.json
</details>
<details>
<summary>Villager Outposts</summary>
New Structures that can be found throughout your world. There are 7 different types (for each Villager type one) and each one generates with some Villagers.

<details>

</details>
<details>
<summary>Decorative Structures</summary>
You can now find decorative Structures in various biomes.
</details>
<details>
<summary>Bundles</summary>

<details>
<summary>Wrench</summary>
Craft a Wrench from 2 Iron Ingots and a Stick. It rotates most blocks and is compatible with Unbreaking and Mending.
</details>
<details>
<summary>Soul-/Campfires</summary>
Soul-/Campfire got overhauled and can now be crafted as either lit or unlit variants. They also stay lit/unlit when broken.
</details>
<details>

Armor Stands now always have arms. You can also get a small armor stand by converting one in a Stonecutter.

</details>
<details>

## 🛠️ How to, Instructions & Commands
The datapack usually just needs to be installed. There is nothing else you have to do. It just works out of the box.

But if you are playing on a PaperMC Server (or a PaperMC Fork like Purpur) make sure to set allow-non-player-entities-on-scoreboards in your paper config files to true in order for everything to work properly.

**The data pack version also double functions as a resource pack. So in order to be able to see them, just place the data pack .zip also in your resource folder and activate it like any other resource pack.**
