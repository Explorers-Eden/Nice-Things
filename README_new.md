## 📖 Description
**Nice Things** adds a bunch of new stuff to the game: blocks, items, recipes, structures and lots of small tweaks to make the game a bit more fun. From Conveyors to climbable chains, there's plenty to play around with! Suggestions are always welcome.

<br>

## 📋 Features
<details>
<summary>🧱 New Blocks</summary>

- **Display Case**: shows off and rotates any item you put in. Only the player who placed it can use or break it. 17 variants
- **Conveyor**: moves items and entities in one direction. Items carried through water turn Dead Coral into Coral, Concrete Powder into Concrete and Dirt into Mud
- **Fan**: blows items and entities in the direction it's facing and ages Copper Blocks it fires
- **Chopper**: works like a Conveyor, but also breaks blocks down, e.g. Stone into Cobblestone, Logs into Stripped Logs and Ore Blocks into ores
- **Container**: stores up to 10,000 stackable items of one type and doubles as a neat item filter

</details>

<details>
<summary>🔧 New Items</summary>

- **Wrench**: rotates most blocks; works with Unbreaking and Mending
- **Emerald on a Stick**: Villagers nearby follow you around, and using it makes them jump
- **Kaleidoscope**: look through it for a colorful view of the world
- **Biome Brews**: potions that change the biome in an 8×8×8 area around you
- **Armadillo Chestplate**: removes Poison from the wearer
- **Sculk & Simple Harnesses**: two new Harness variants
- **Ambient Music Discs**: two new discs with Forest and Ocean ambience, crafted from Disc Fragments
- **New Paintings** and **Banner Patterns** for every number and letter (0-9, A-Z)
- **New Food**: Boiled Eggs, Cheese (clears all effects), Apple & Chorus Pie, Sweet Berry Cookies, Cake Slices and Golden Sweet Berries, Potatoes, Chorus Fruits & Beetroots

</details>

<details>
<summary>🏰 New Structures</summary>

- **Villager Outposts**: one for each Villager type, each with Villagers living there
- **End Ships**: crashed and flying ships in the End, each with at least one broken Elytra
- **Decorative Structures** in desert, jungle, ocean, snowy and taiga biomes

</details>

<details>
<summary>📜 New Recipes</summary>

- Craft Bells, Bundles (from Leather or Rabbit Hide), Chainmail Armor, all Horse Armor, Name Tags, Gilded Blackstone, Rooted Dirt, Pale Moss Blocks/Carpets and lit or unlit (Soul) Campfires
- Craft Invisible (Glow) Item Frames from a Frame and a Glass Pane
- Turn White Wool back into String and Saplings into Sticks
- Repair Chipped and Damaged Anvils with Iron Blocks
- Downgrade Blue Ice to Packed Ice and Packed Ice to Ice
- Add or remove the flowers of (Flowering) Azalea Leaves
- Duplicate Pottery Sherds with a Brick
- Redye Glass, Glass Panes, Terracotta, Glazed Terracotta, Concrete, Concrete Powder and Candles
- New ways to get Brown Dye, Green Dye, Leather (from Rotten Flesh) and Dead Bushes
- **Stonecutter**: pick a specific Painting, make Mini Blocks, get wooden items from Logs and turn Armor Stands into small ones

</details>

<details>
<summary>🎨 Cosmetics</summary>

- Dye your **Saddles** and **Elytra**
- Give **Copper Golems** items to wear on their rods by throwing them the item ([full list](https://github.com/Explorers-Eden/Nice-Things/blob/main/data/minecraft/tags/item/shearable_from_copper_golem.json))

</details>

<details>
<summary>⚙️ Gameplay Tweaks</summary>

- Climb Iron/Copper Chains and Big Dripleaves
- Honey Blocks reset fall damage, and (Pale) Moss Carpets dampen vibrations
- Armor Stands always have arms
- (Soul) Campfires stay lit or unlit when broken
- Trapdoors and Carpets no longer block Bookshelves from an Enchanting Table, and Chiseled Bookshelves count too
- Endermen can only pick up Melons and Pumpkins, keeping your builds safe
- Striders stay warm on Magma Blocks, (Soul) Campfires and (Soul) Fire
- Turtles eat Kelp, and Axolotls eat Tropical Fish (no bucket needed)
- Light Sculk or Crying Obsidian to get Soul Fire
- Place Small Dripleaves on all dirt types and Farmland
- The four unused Paintings (Wind, Fire, Earth, Water) are now placeable

</details>

<details>
<summary>⛏️ Mining Tweaks</summary>

- Mine Ender Chests without Silk Touch
- Mine Frogspawn, Budding Amethyst and Reinforced Deepslate with Silk Touch
- Faster mining with **Axes** (Heads, Beds, Candles, Cactus, Honeycomb Blocks), **Hoes** (Froglights, Redstone Lamps, Sea Lanterns) and **Pickaxes** (Glass, Glass Panes, Beacons, Frosted Ice, Levers)

</details>

<br>

## 🛠️ Technical Gibberish
This data pack works out of the box and also works as the **required** resource pack. Just put the same file into your `resourcepacks` folder and enable it in game so the textures show up properly.

_(Not needed for the auto-converted mod versions in Single-Player.)_

**PaperMC users:** enable `allow-non-player-entities-on-scoreboards` in your Paper config so everything works properly.
